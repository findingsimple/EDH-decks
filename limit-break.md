## Notes
**Strategy:** Suit up Cloud and friends with Equipment, then attack every turn. Cloud draws you a card for each equipped attacker and makes two Treasures whenever he attacks with 7+ power, so the deck snowballs: attack → draw more gear → equip more attackers → repeat.

**How to play (game plan):**
1. **Turns 1–3 — set up.** Play ramp (Cultivate, Nature's Lore, Rampant Growth, Sword of the Animist) and cheap enablers: Puresteel Paladin (draw off every Equipment + free equips at 3 artifacts), Cid (Equipment costs {1} less), Stoneforge Mystic (fetch the perfect Equipment), Adelbert Steiner (grows with every Equipment).
2. **Turn 4–5 — Cloud arrives.** Cast Cloud with at least one Equipment already on the battlefield — his ETB attaches one for **free**. Lightning Greaves or Hero's Heirloom means he can attack the same turn.
3. **Every turn after — attack with equipped creatures.** Not just Cloud! He draws a card for **each** equipped attacking creature, so spread spare gear around before combat and swing wide.
4. **Snowball.** Spend the draws and Treasures on more Equipment and threats. Keep one protection card ready for board wipes (see below).

**Getting Cloud to 7 power** (turns on Treasures — he's a 4/4, so he needs +3):
- Buster Sword (+3/+2 → exactly 7, plus draw + free spell when he connects)
- Colossus Hammer (+10/+10 for {1} — the dream Stoneforge fetch)
- Ultima Weapon (+7/+7 and kills a blocker every attack)
- Wrecking Ball Arm (base 7/7, dodges small blockers)
- Sephiroth's attack trigger (modified creatures become 7/5 for the turn)

**The 7-power club** (other payoffs once someone hits 7+):
- Tifa: 7+ power creature connects → untap the team and take an **extra combat**
- Vincent at 7+ power: his combat damage hits **every** opponent
- Bugenhagen: draw an extra card every upkeep

**Cheap/free attach tricks** (equip costs add up — dodge them):
- Cloud's ETB, Hero's Blade (auto-attaches to any legend), Barret, Avalanche Leader (attaches at combat, plus a 2/2 Rebel per Equipment), Amy Rose (attaches on attack)
- Puresteel Paladin with 3+ artifacts = **all equips are free**
- Helitrooper and Cloud, Planet's Champion make equips {2} cheaper
- Zack Fair dies so someone else inherits his gear, counters, and indestructible
- Stoneforge cheats the Equipment **onto the battlefield** ({1}{W}, instant speed) but doesn't attach it — pair with the tricks above

**Protection — hold one up when the board is scary:**
- Champion's Helm / Darksteel Plate / Bastion Protector make Cloud very hard to kill
- Clever Concealment (often free via convoke), Ultimate Magic: Holy, Teferi's Protection dodge board wipes
- Cast Ultimate Magic: Holy from foretell exile and it also prevents all damage to you this turn

**Removal — what kills what:**
- Dispatch (exile a creature with 3 artifacts out), Cloud's Limit Break (kills tapped creatures — best right after someone else attacks), Chaos Warp (fixes any problem permanent — save it for the scariest thing), Ultima Weapon (kills a blocker every attack)
- Vandalblast / Lorehold Charm / Decimate hit artifacts — Vandalblast (even overloaded) **never hits your own**
- Vanquish the Horde / Austere Command reset the board — but they hit **your** creatures too; wipe first, rebuild with Unfinished Business (returns a creature plus two Equipment attached)

**Mana tricks:**
- Inspiring Statuary: tap idle Equipment to help cast **nonartifact** spells (improvise)
- Jeska's Will: big red mana burst (+ exile 3 to play) — fuels a double-equip turn
- Summoning Materia: cast creatures off the top of your library
- Elspeth, Storm Slayer doubles ALL your tokens — including Cloud's Treasures and Barret's Rebels

**Win conditions:**
- Voltron: 21 commander damage from an equipped Cloud (Trailblazer's Boots = usually unblockable; Rogue's Passage as backup)
- Go wide: several equipped attackers + Tifa extra combats
- Barret Wallace pings the defending player per equipped creature; Vincent splashes damage to everyone
- Buster Sword free-casting your expensive spells every hit

**Watch-outs:**
- Equipping is **sorcery-speed only** (your main phase) — plan before combat
- Colossus Hammer removes flying; don't put it on Helitrooper expecting to fly in
- Cloud's Limit Break kills **all** tapped creatures on the Omnislash tier — your attackers are tapped too
- Decimate must have all four targets (artifact, creature, enchantment, land) — you can't cast it otherwise
- Skullclamp's +1/−1 kills your 1-toughness tokens (Samurai's Katana Hero, Soldiers) — that's usually the plan: draw 2!
